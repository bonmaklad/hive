## Required before MVP scope lock

- Client approval of the high-level requirements.
- Resolution of open decisions needed for the first release.
- Approved product name and initial brand direction.
- Confirmation of the free/paid boundary and pricing status.
- Technical validation of property data, authentication, payment, email and storage choices.
- Agreement on privacy, legal, security and accessibility release gates.

## Recommended delivery sequence

1. Approve this requirements pack.
2. Resolve MVP-critical open decisions.
3. Validate third-party data and technology assumptions.
4. Produce information architecture and low-fidelity flows.
5. Convert requirements and BDD scenarios into the delivery backlog.
6. Agree measurable quality and performance targets.
7. Complete visual design using the approved brand pack.
8. Build and test release slices against stable IDs.
9. Complete legal, privacy, security and launch-readiness reviews.
10. Pilot with representative homeowners, investors and renovators before nationwide promotion.
