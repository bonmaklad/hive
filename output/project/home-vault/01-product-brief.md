> Product name: Home Vault (working title)

## Executive summary

Home Vault is a proposed mobile-first web service that gives New Zealand homeowners, renovators and small property investors one visual, structured place to retain the information, documents, reminders, tasks, assets and costs associated with each property. The initial proposition replaces fragmented spreadsheets, email, calendar entries, paper receipts, informal contact names and physical reminders such as retained paint cans. A free one-property record is intended to build reach, while a paid plan adds active property, maintenance, renovation, financial and reporting tools. The longer-term opportunity is a buyer-safe property passport and ownership transfer experience that preserves useful property knowledge without disclosing a seller's confidential renovation economics.

## Customer segments

### SEG-01 - Small-portfolio residential property investors

**Priority:** Primary

New Zealand owners with more than one property, typically two to nine properties, who manage property knowledge themselves rather than using specialist portfolio software.

**Needs**

- A reliable record for each property and room
- Maintenance and warranty reminders
- Asset, invoice, expense and renovation-cost visibility
- Information and exports suitable for accountants

### SEG-02 - Renovators and property flippers

**Priority:** Primary adjacent

People actively improving and reselling property who need room-level photos, tasks, budgets, costs, assets and before-and-after records.

**Needs**

- Room-level renovation records
- Budget and actual-cost tracking
- Before-and-after imagery
- A future buyer-safe handover that protects margin and cost details

### SEG-03 - Owner-occupiers

**Priority:** Reach and future conversion

Single-property homeowners who need a simple home information record and may later renovate, acquire another property or upgrade to active management tools.

**Needs**

- One place for paint colours, photos and property documents
- A simple, low-friction free experience
- A path to paid reminders, assets and reporting when needs grow

### SEG-04 - Property collaborators

**Priority:** Supporting

Partners, family members and other trusted people invited by an owner to help view or manage property information.

**Needs**

- Clear admin and read-only permissions
- Shared notes and consistent information
- Controlled access to sensitive financial records

### SEG-05 - Property buyers, sellers and agents

**Priority:** Future

Participants in a property sale who may use a paid property passport and ownership-claim process.

**Needs**

- Useful room, asset and warranty information
- Clear provenance and ownership transfer
- Protection of the seller's confidential renovation and purchase economics

## Market position

Home Vault sits between unstructured personal tools and professional property-management platforms. It is intended to be easier and more valuable than a spreadsheet for self-managing owners, without requiring the complexity of enterprise or agency property-management software.

### Current alternatives

- Memory and informal knowledge
- Physical reminders such as retained paint cans and paper receipts
- Email and text-message search
- Personal calendars
- Google Docs, PDFs and spreadsheets
- Generic CRM tools
- Specialist property-management software used by larger portfolios

### Differentiators

- Property-to-room-to-asset information hierarchy
- Image-rich, mobile-first experience
- Documents, tasks, reminders and financial records in one place
- Owner and accountant reporting
- Future privacy-aware property passport and transfer

## Problems and impact

| ID | Problem | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PROB-01 | Property information is fragmented across memory, paper, email, texts, documents, spreadsheets and calendars. | Owners waste time searching and cannot rely on a complete record. |
| PROB-02 | Owners lose practical knowledge such as paint colours, product details and which trade completed work. | Maintenance and repeat work are slower, less accurate and more expensive. |
| PROB-03 | Warranties, servicing dates and recurring maintenance are easy to miss. | Owners may lose warranty value, defer maintenance or incur avoidable costs. |
| PROB-04 | Asset, invoice and depreciation information is difficult to assemble for accountants. | Financial reporting is incomplete and owners may miss legitimate depreciation information. |
| PROB-05 | Renovation costs and ongoing property expenses are maintained in separate spreadsheets or not tracked consistently. | Owners cannot easily understand spend by property, room, category or period. |
| PROB-06 | Existing tools are either too manual or designed for larger professional property operations. | Small-portfolio owners lack a right-sized product that is worth paying for. |
| PROB-07 | Useful knowledge is lost when a property is sold, while sellers do not want to disclose confidential renovation economics. | Buyers receive an incomplete handover and sellers face privacy and commercial concerns. |
| PROB-08 | Property records are often text-heavy and administrative rather than visual and engaging. | Owners are less likely to keep records current or celebrate and share renovation progress. |

## Intended outcomes

- Create one trusted source of property knowledge for each owner.
- Reduce the time and effort needed to find property, room, asset, trade and document information.
- Protect value by keeping warranties, service dates and recurring maintenance visible.
- Make renovation and ownership costs understandable by property, room, category and period.
- Make accountant handover and asset/depreciation preparation easier.
- Provide an image-rich experience that encourages regular use and supports before-and-after storytelling.
- Build a large free-user base with a credible path to paid subscriptions and a future strategic property-platform partnership or acquisition.

## Product principles

- The product must provide clearer value and be easier to use than a spreadsheet.
- The information hierarchy must remain intuitive: portfolio, property, room, asset or record.
- Capturing a photo or document should be fast on a mobile browser.
- The free experience must be genuinely useful for one property; paid value must come from active management, automation and reporting.
- The design must be visual, modern and mobile-first while remaining a responsive web service for the initial release.
- Customer behaviour will inform later changes; the scope is expected to adapt after real-world validation.
- Private financial information must not be exposed through collaboration or future ownership transfer.
