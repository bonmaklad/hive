## Detailed discovery questions

1. Is the preferred core portfolio description two to nine residential properties, or should no numeric range be published?
2. Are renovators and property flippers a co-primary segment or a secondary segment?
3. Exactly which records can a free customer create, upload and retain, and what storage limit applies?
4. How many properties are included in the paid plan?
5. Are basic manual tasks available on the free plan, or are all tasks a paid capability?
6. Can a free customer record a simple appliance, or is the asset register entirely paid?
7. Are user invitations and comments required in the MVP?
8. Is collaboration account-wide, portfolio-wide or property-specific?
9. Which financial records can administrators and read-only collaborators see?
10. What authentication, email verification and password policy will apply?
11. Which payment service will be used, and what are the trial, cancellation, refund, tax and failed-payment rules?
12. Which New Zealand property-data source, licence and fallback behaviour are approved?
13. Will external calendar synchronisation be required, or will the MVP use only the Home Vault calendar?
14. What reminder lead times, recurrence rules, recipient controls, timezone behaviour and escalation rules are required?
15. Which file types, size limits, storage limits, retention, deletion, export and backup rules are required?
16. Will uploaded bills be stored only, manually transcribed or processed using OCR in a later release?
17. Which IRD-aligned asset and depreciation fields are required, and what accounting disclaimer is approved?
18. Should accountant output be CSV, spreadsheet, PDF or multiple formats?
19. Is before-and-after social sharing included in the initial release, and which privacy controls are required?
20. Which warranty documents can transfer to a buyer, and how are confidential prices redacted if the document is needed as evidence?
21. Does a former owner retain an immutable historical accounting record after transfer?
22. How will a future buyer's right to claim a property be verified?
23. What data-hosting location, monitoring, analytics, recovery and support expectations apply?
24. Which security, privacy and legal reviews are mandatory release gates?
25. What accessibility standard and supported-browser matrix will be approved?
