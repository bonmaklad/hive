# Scope and roadmap

> Status: Draft for client approval  
> Version: 0.1  
> Prepared: 15 July 2026  
> Product name: Home Vault (working title)

## Initial release

- Public website, registration, authentication and profile
- Free and paid access model
- Property and room setup
- Room photos, paint/finish records and documents
- Assets, invoices, warranties and service information
- Tasks, recurring reminders, email notifications, calendar and Kanban views
- Renovation costs, ongoing expenses, categories, budgets and twelve-month views
- Property, room and accountant reporting/exports
- Admin/read-only invitations, comments and preferences
- Responsive mobile-first web experience
- Terms, privacy, access control and pre-launch security assurance

## Post-MVP

- Social-ready before-and-after sharing
- Expanded system-generated maintenance prompts
- Further reporting refinements based on customer behaviour
- Additional approved integrations

## Future

- Separately chargeable buyer-safe Property Passport
- Ownership transfer and new-owner claim
- Barcode and QR-code product capture
- AI-assisted paint, image and product metadata
- Council-change and annual-audit prompts
- Energy and solar insights with transparent supplier referrals
- Invitations or workflows for trades

## Explicitly outside the initial release

- Native iOS or Android applications
- Trade-user invitations and trade workflow
- Automated legal, tax, depreciation or accounting advice
- Automatic direct import of utility, gas or rates data; the workshop agreed document upload for the initial concept
- Buyer-facing ownership transfer and Property Passport release
- AI image analysis, barcode/QR lookup and product-price lookup
- Automatic council-change monitoring and supplier referral marketplace
- Company formation, shareholding arrangements and social-account administration
- A committed delivery date before approval and sizing

## Scope rule

Future ideas do not enter the MVP unless they are explicitly approved, sized and assigned to a release.
